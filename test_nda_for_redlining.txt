MODIFIED NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made effective January 22, 2026, by and between:

TechStart Inc., a California corporation ("Company"), and
John Smith ("Recipient").

BACKGROUND
The Parties wish to explore a business opportunity, and Company may disclose confidential information to Recipient.

AGREEMENT

1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

1.1 Definition. "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by Company to Recipient that is marked "Confidential" or should reasonably be considered confidential. This includes: technical data, trade secrets, research, products, customers, software, and business information.

1.2 Marking. Confidential Information disclosed orally must be confirmed in writing within forty-five (45) days.

2. EXCLUSIONS

Information is not confidential if it:
(a) was already known to Recipient;
(b) becomes publicly known through no wrongful act;
(c) is received from a third party without breach;
(d) is independently developed by Recipient; or
(e) must be disclosed by law with prompt notice to Company.

3. RECIPIENT OBLIGATIONS

3.1 Non-Use and Non-Disclosure. Recipient will not use Confidential Information except to evaluate a business relationship. Recipient will not disclose it to third parties.

3.2 Standard of Care. Recipient shall protect Confidential Information with reasonable care, at minimum the same care used for its own confidential information.

3.3 Limited Disclosure. Recipient may share Confidential Information only with employees and contractors who need to know and are bound by similar confidentiality obligations.

4. TERM

4.1 Duration. This Agreement remains in effect for four (4) years from the Effective Date, unless terminated with forty-five (45) days written notice.

4.2 Continuing Obligations. Obligations survive termination for four (4) years.

4.3 Return of Materials. Upon termination, Recipient shall return or destroy all Confidential Information within fifteen (15) days.

5. NO RIGHTS GRANTED

All Confidential Information remains Company property. No license is granted. Company retains all intellectual property rights.

6. NO WARRANTY

CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION IS PROVIDED "AS IS" WITHOUT ANY WARRANTIES.

7. REMEDIES

7.1 Equitable Relief. Company may seek injunctive relief for breaches.

7.2 Attorneys' Fees. Prevailing party recovers reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

8. MISCELLANEOUS

8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by California law.

8.2 Dispute Resolution. Disputes resolved through arbitration in Los Angeles, California.

8.3 Entire Agreement. This is the complete agreement between the Parties.

8.4 Amendments. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

8.5 Severability. Invalid provisions will be reformed to be enforceable.

8.6 No Assignment. Recipient may not assign without consent. Company may assign to successors.

8.7 Waiver. Waivers must be in writing.

8.8 Counterparts. May be executed in counterparts.
